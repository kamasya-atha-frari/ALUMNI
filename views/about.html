Department of Management Information Systems

Yönetim Bilişim Sistemleri (YBS) • Beyazıt, Istanbul

Established within the historic Faculty of Economics at Istanbul University, the Department of Management Information Systems integrates computational science, analytical modeling, and strategic business leadership.

Our curriculum synthesizes enterprise database administration, software architecture, cloud platforms, and data analytics. Through our alumni ecosystem, we track graduates across major technology, consulting, finance, and telecommunication sectors globally.
