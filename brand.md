# Brand Identity: AlumniIstü (ALUMNI)

> High-end dark booking aesthetic with navy depths and coral illumination.

## Brand Vision & Tone
**AlumniIstü (ALUMNI)** embraces a *midnight dark mode* aesthetic inspired by exclusive digital platforms and financial vaults. Built upon a deep **Navy** canvas, warm **Cream** editorial typography, and an illuminated **Coral** accent, the interface delivers an elegant, high-trust interactive focus. Display headings are set in *Ivy Presto* / *Playfair Display* to project institutional prestige for Istanbul University's Department of Management Information Systems (MIS / YBS).

## Core Color Palette
- **Navy (`#14213D`)**: Primary page canvas, dark-mode foundational background, and primary card surface depth.
- **Cream (`#F4EDE0`)**: Primary editorial text, high-emphasis data labels, and eye-friendly readable typography.
- **Coral (`#FF6B58`)**: Primary chromatic accent, Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons, category eyebrows, interactive indicators, and subtle illumination.

## Typography Principles
- **Display & Headings (`≥28px`)**: *Ivy Presto* / *Playfair Display* (weights `400`, `500`) with subtle positive letter-spacing (`0.01em`) for hero headlines, section titles, and large numerical proof points.
- **UI & Body Text (`<28px`)**: *Inter* (weights `300`, `400`, `500`, `600`, `700`) for navigation, body copy, pill controls, table cells, and metadata.
