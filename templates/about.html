About AlumniNexus

AlumniNexus is an institutional talent intelligence platform engineered for graduates, faculty, and students of Istanbul University Faculty of Economics, Department of Management Information Systems (Yönetim Bilişim Sistemleri - YBS).

Modern Architecture

Pure PHP 8.2 backend adhering to strict typing (`declare(strict_types=1);`), containerized with Docker and MySQL 8.0 with durable named volumes passing the Forgetting Test.

Career & Applications

End-to-end recruitment pipeline connecting graduates with technology enterprises like Turkcell, Trendyol, and Getir, complete with candidate review workflow.
