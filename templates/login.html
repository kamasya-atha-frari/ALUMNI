Token Lifecycle & Server-Side Logout

  • Login (`POST /api/auth/login`): Verifies credentials against `password_hash` and issues a JWT containing `sub`, `username`, `role`, and unique `jti`.
  • Authorized Requests: Client sends `Authorization: Bearer <token>` on protected routes like `GET /api/auth/me`.
  • Logout (`POST /api/auth/logout`): Revokes the token's `jti` in MySQL `revoked_tokens`, clears local storage, and blocks any reuse (`401 TOKEN_REVOKED`).
